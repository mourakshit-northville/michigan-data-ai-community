TWO GROUPS + ONE CONFERENCE

Different formats.
Shared curiosity.

Choose the event and community that fits how you learn, connect and contribute. The two meetup groups have separate official registration platforms, while Day of Data Detroit is a conference program. We have not formed additional chapters or staffed a regional team.

01 / ANNUAL CONFERENCE

Day of Data Detroit

A full-day gathering for engineers, analysts, data architects, BI professionals, students and technologists. Topics include SQL, Fabric, Databricks, cloud and applied AI.
