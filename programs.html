How the programs work together

The website is the permanent home. Platforms are distribution channels.

Members should be able to discover a program here, join the community mailing list here, and then use Eventbrite, Luma, Meetup or Databricks Community only where a specific event needs those services.

One community calendarAll recurring programs and major events are surfaced together.
One mailing relationshipNewsletter membership is not tied to a single ticketing platform.
Shared sponsors & partnersOrganizations can support a meetup, a program or the broader community.
Shared volunteers & speakersCommunity participation can span multiple programs.
