Our mission

Build a stronger local ecosystem for practical data and AI learning.

The community brings together practitioners, students, educators, speakers, employers, universities, sponsors and technology partners. Core community events are designed to remain free whenever venue and sponsor support makes that possible.

Practical learningReal implementation experience, demos, architecture, engineering and lessons learned.
Cross-platformDatabricks, Microsoft Fabric, SQL, cloud, open source and emerging AI technologies.
Accessible locallyDetroit, Ann Arbor and Southeast Michigan venues with virtual participation when practical.
Community-ledSpeakers, volunteers, partners and members help shape future programming.
