Why support the community

Put your organization in the room with Michigan’s data and AI practitioners.

Sponsors and community partners help fund venue space, refreshments, event materials, speaker support, student participation and the operating costs behind recurring community programs. In return, we focus on useful, transparent recognition rather than clutter: clear sponsor visibility, event acknowledgement and a durable public record of support.

3community programs share one regional home: Day of Data Detroit, the Detroit Southeast Michigan Databricks User Group, and cross-platform Michigan Data & AI programming.

DAY OF DATA DETROIT

501(c)(3) sponsorship processing

SQL Saturday Corporation is a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. The official Day of Data Detroit sponsorship plan states that when an organization requires a nonprofit invoice or sponsorship receipt, sponsorship processing through SQL Saturday Corporation can be coordinated with the event organizer.

That gives Day of Data Detroit a clear nonprofit sponsorship path for eligible event-specific support. Organizations interested in that route should contact Mou Rakshit so the sponsorship can be coordinated through the official Day of Data process.

Important distinction: Michigan Data & AI Community itself is not represented on this site as a 501(c)(3), and general community contributions are not described as tax-deductible charitable gifts. A broader fiscal-sponsorship arrangement for Michigan Data & AI Community would require a separate written agreement or approval.
