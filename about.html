About the community

An independent, year-round home for Michigan data and AI learning

Michigan Data & AI Community was founded by Mou Rakshit to connect recurring meetups, platform user groups, technical conferences, speakers, students, volunteers and partners under one durable regional identity.

Mission

Make practical technical learning easier to access locally.

The community serves practitioners, students, educators, technology companies, employers and speakers across Detroit, Ann Arbor and Southeast Michigan. Core programming is designed to remain free whenever sponsorship and venue support make that possible.

PracticalArchitecture, engineering, analytics and AI sessions grounded in real implementation experience.
Cross-platformDatabricks, Microsoft Fabric, SQL, cloud, open source and emerging AI tools.
AccessibleFree community events, student outreach and career/certification guidance.
LocalBuilding stronger connections among Michigan practitioners, employers, universities and speakers.

Founder & lead organizer

Mou Rakshit

Mou is a Data & AI architect, speaker, instructor and community organizer. She founded Michigan Data & AI Community, Day of Data Detroit and the Detroit Southeast Michigan Databricks User Group, and organizes the Southeast Michigan Data & AI Community. Her public technical work spans Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, enterprise AI, governance, conversational BI and data engineering.
