About the community

An independent home for year-round data and AI learning in Michigan.

Michigan Data & AI Community connects recurring meetups, technical user groups, Day of Data Detroit, speakers, students, volunteers, universities, sponsors and technology partners under one regional identity.

Our mission

Make practical technical learning easier to access locally.

The community serves practitioners, students, educators, employers, speakers and technology partners across Detroit, Ann Arbor and Southeast Michigan. Core programming is designed to remain free whenever sponsorship and venue support make that possible.

PracticalArchitecture, engineering, analytics and AI sessions grounded in real implementation experience.
Cross-platformDatabricks, Microsoft Fabric, SQL, cloud, open source and emerging AI tools.
AccessibleFree community events, student outreach and career/certification guidance.
RegionalConnecting practitioners and organizations across Detroit, Ann Arbor and Southeast Michigan.

Governance & administration

The community has a clear administrator and an independent identity.

Michigan Data & AI Community is not managed by Eventbrite, Luma, Meetup, Databricks or another event platform. Those services may support individual events, but the community identity, domain, event calendar, partnerships and member communications are administered independently.
