About us

A welcoming place for practical data and AI learning.

Michigan Data & AI Community connects students, engineers, analysts, educators, speakers and organizations across Detroit, Ann Arbor and Southeast Michigan.

What we do

Share skills, opportunities and experience.

We organize meetups, technical demos, conference sessions, professional development and certification guidance for the region.

Our programming spans Microsoft Fabric, Databricks, Power BI, SQL, streaming, analytics, data engineering, governance and AI. Our goal is practical, accessible community education.

Founder and organizer

Mou Rakshit

Mou is a data and AI platform architect, instructor and speaker with more than 25 years in technology. She organizes regional community programs, including Day of Data Detroit and Detroit Databricks learning activities.

Her personal technical writing and career portfolio are maintained separately.
