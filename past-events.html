17 NOVEMBER 2026 · JW MARRIOTT, WASHINGTON, DC

Enterprise Search & Discovery 2026

On November 17, 2026, Michigan Data & AI Community founder Mou Rakshit presented “Conversational Search, Governed Data Discovery, and Quality Research” at Enterprise Search & Discovery 2026 at the JW Marriott in Washington, DC, alongside Jonathan Thill of EBSCO. The session explored how enterprise search is evolving toward trusted answers across structured and unstructured information, with practical attention to context, evidence and governance. Enterprise Search & Discovery was one of five co-located KMWorld 2026 events. These original photographs show Mou’s presentation, the shared session and moments after the talk.
