DATABRICKS USER GROUP · CERTIFICATION

Databricks Certification Roadmap
All Tracks and a 30-Day Plan

Following strong interest in our previous certification meetup, this expanded virtual session covers the major Databricks certification tracks, official exam guides, and a practical 30-day preparation plan.

WHAT TO EXPECT · 60 MINUTES

Choose the right Databricks certification track.

Following requests for more certification guidance at our previous meetup, this session takes a broader look at the Databricks certification portfolio. We will compare exam paths, review official preparation resources, and build a realistic study plan. Time is reserved for questions.

  1. Choose an examData Engineer (Associate and Professional), Data Analyst Associate, Machine Learning Engineer (Associate and Professional), Generative AI Engineer Associate, Context Engineer Associate, and Apache Spark Developer Associate
  2. Explore official materialCurrent Databricks exam guides, Academy training, Free Edition practice ideas, and study resources
  3. Create a 30-day planA realistic 30-day study plan with weekly topics, hands-on exercises, and practice-question reviews
  4. Career Q&AExam readiness, renewal considerations, real-world learning, and participant questions

Who should join?

Students, data engineers, analysts, architects, career changers and anyone considering this certification path. No exam voucher or job placement is promised.
